Getting around

Boca has no car for me. Everything runs on local buses and water taxis.

Boca sits where the coast road ends its run south of Puerto Vallarta. Past Boca there is no road along the shore: Colomitos, Las Ánimas, Quimixto, Majahuitas and Yelapa are reached on foot or by boat.

Puerto Vallarta ↔ Boca

Buses every 15 to 20 minutes from Basilio Badillo and Constitución in the Zona Romántica. About 35 to 45 minutes, roughly 10 to 15 pesos. The windshield says "Boca" or "Mismaloya / Boca". The last bus back is around 10:15 pm.

Boca → El Tuito

The blue "El Tuito / Botanical Gardens" bus, from the southbound side of the highway. Every 20 to 30 minutes, about 45 minutes, roughly 25 to 35 pesos.

Boca ↔ Yelapa

Water taxis about every hour from 8 am, roughly 30 minutes. The last boat back leaves around 6 pm and often fills up.
