An afternoon water taxi down the coast to Yelapa, a village with no road in.

The plan

  • Water taxi from Boca, about 30 minutes
  • Explore the beach and the village
  • Saturday music at the Yacht Club in the pueblo
  • Watch for flyers on poles for other gigs
  • Sunset drums and bonfires on the beach

Good to know

Yelapa runs on cash and foot traffic. Pangas come and go from the beach and the north-end pier.
