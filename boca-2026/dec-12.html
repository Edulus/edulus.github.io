Día de la Virgen de Guadalupe

7891011121314151617181920212223

The biggest day of the Fiestas Guadalupanas, and the anniversary of Puerto Vallarta's founding.

The plan

  • Morning: pick up the leather bag before the crowds
  • From about 11 am: the pilgrimage down Juárez Street to the Guadalupe church
  • Evening: food and music at Plaza de Armas by the Malecón
  • Aim for a bus around 9 pm, ahead of the crowds

Good to know

For twelve days leading up to December 12, groups from workplaces, neighbourhoods and churches walk in procession to the church, many in traditional dress, with music and dancers. December 12 is the peak.
