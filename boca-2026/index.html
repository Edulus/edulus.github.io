Boca de Tomatlán

7 to 23 December 2026. A slow stay in a river-mouth fishing village south of Puerto Vallarta, where the jungle comes down to the sea.

Hi Valerie

This is where I'll be in December. Home base is Casa Periquitos at Casa Cereza, a small apartment above the village. The plan is simple: get to know Boca, swim in the river, read, and eat locally. Trips into Puerto Vallarta, a Sunday market in the hills and a night in Yelapa are the extras.

Each day below has its own page with the plan and some photos of the places.
