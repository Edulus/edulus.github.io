Boca de Tomatlán

Dec. 7 to 23, 2026

A slow stay in a river-mouth fishing village south of Puerto Vallarta, where the jungle comes down to the sea.

The trip

Home base is Casa Periquitos at Casa Cereza, a one-bedroom apartment with a garden balcony, reached by a footbridge over the river. The plan is simple: get to know Boca, swim in the river, read, and eat locally. Trips into Puerto Vallarta, a Sunday market in the hills and a night in Yelapa are the extras.

Each day below has its own page with the plan and some photos of the places.

Where it is

Boca sits where the coast road ends its run south of Puerto Vallarta. Past Boca there is no road along the shore: Colomitos, Las Ánimas, Quimixto, Majahuitas and Yelapa are reached on foot or by boat.

Where I'm staying

Casa Cereza

A small garden property across the river from the village, reached on foot over a pedestrian bridge. Cherry and Nick, from San Diego, came to Boca for years before buying the place, and they live on site.

There are three apartments, each with its own look: the Jungle Hideaway, the Bungalow, and Casa Periquitos. Periquitos is a studio with a private balcony over a garden of banana and papaya trees, where iguanas sometimes turn up. At night you see the village lights and houses against the jungle hills.
