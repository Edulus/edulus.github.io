A daytime food tour, a free afternoon in Centro, then the Wednesday night gallery walk.

The plan

  • Vallarta Food Tours, about 3 hours, late morning or early afternoon
  • Coffee, the Malecón, wandering Centro
  • Vallarta ArtWalk, 6 to 10 pm, galleries in the Centro art district
  • Leave by 9:30 for the last bus to Boca

Good to know

The ArtWalk runs every Wednesday evening in season. Galleries open their doors, often with a drink and the artists on hand.
