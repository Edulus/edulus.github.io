Food tour and Art Walk

A daytime food tour, a free afternoon in Centro, then the Wednesday night gallery walk.

The plan

  • Vallarta Food Tours, about 3 hours, late morning or early afternoon. The Original Downtown Tour covers 8 regional dishes in Centro and the Zona Romántica; the Old Town Taco Tour does 7 tastings
  • Coffee, the Malecón, wandering Centro
  • Vallarta ArtWalk, 6 to 10 pm, galleries in the Centro Histórico
  • Pick up the gallery map at any participating gallery
  • Leave by 9:30 for the last bus to Boca
Vallarta Food Tours ↗Vallarta ArtWalk ↗

Good to know

The ArtWalk runs every Wednesday from Oct 28, 2026 to May 26, 2027, its 30th season. It is self-guided: walk between the galleries at your own pace and meet the artists. Food tours include 6 to 8 tastings and a local guide.
