The Las Ánimas hike

7891011121314151617181920212223

A morning on the coastal trail from Boca to Las Ánimas beach. It starts right outside Casa Cereza.

The plan

  • Early start, before the heat
  • Trail south along the jungle coast, about 4 km
  • Swim stop at Colomitos cove on the way
  • Lunch on the beach at Las Ánimas
  • Water taxi back to Boca, or walk if there's energy left
  • Easy afternoon by the river

Good to know

The trail climbs about 230 m and takes under two hours one way. Sturdy shoes and at least a litre of water each. Water taxis back are cash only.
